ABOUT

Business analyst mindset. Data-driven execution.

I’m an Informatics student focused on business analytics, data, and intelligent decision systems. I’m interested in taking ambiguous business problems, breaking them into measurable questions, and using data to determine what is actually happening, why it is happening, and what should happen next.

My work spans SQL and Python-based analysis, KPI and variance analysis, data preparation, statistical and predictive modeling, machine learning, automation, and decision-support systems. I build across the analytical workflow: defining the problem, interrogating the data, identifying meaningful patterns, evaluating models, communicating findings, and translating those findings into actionable recommendations.

I’m especially interested in analytics roles where technical skills are used to solve real business problems—not simply produce reports. I want to understand the business objective, quantify the opportunity or risk, build a defensible analysis, and turn the result into something stakeholders can act on.
