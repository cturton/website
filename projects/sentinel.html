CASE STUDY · MULTI-AGENT DECISION SYSTEM

Sentinel

A governed multi-agent decision system that investigates business signals, assembles evidence, scores confidence, and produces an approval-gated recommendation.

View project ↗← Back to work

THE PROBLEM

Turn a business signal into an auditable decision.

Sentinel is designed around investigation: specialist agents research focused questions, evidence is independently verified, and the findings are synthesized into a decision packet for a human owner.

WHAT I BUILT

Investigation swarm

Five specialist agents work in isolated investigation lanes and submit evidence to an orchestrator.

Evidence graph

Recommendations are connected to source evidence so the reasoning can be inspected.

Confidence scoring

Independent evidence is evaluated before a recommendation is assembled.

Governance controls

Consequential actions remain approval-gated and under human control.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Interactive control-plane prototype combining specialist-agent workflow, decision synthesis, evidence visualization, and a governance ledger.

WHY IT MATTERS

Accelerate investigation without removing accountability.

Sentinel demonstrates a practical pattern for AI-assisted business decisions: agents accelerate research and synthesis while authority boundaries, traceable evidence, and human approval remain explicit.
