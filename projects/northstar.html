CASE STUDY · DECISION INTELLIGENCE

Northstar Decision Intelligence

A business analytics application designed to turn performance data into explainable recommendations, scenario analysis, and clear next actions.

View project on GitHub ↗← Back to work

THE PROBLEM

Move from reporting to decisions.

Traditional KPI dashboards can show what changed without making it obvious what should happen next. Northstar was built around the decision-maker: surface the important variance, identify likely drivers, highlight customer risk, and compare potential actions.

WHAT I BUILT

Executive KPI story

Tracks headline performance and plan variance so the user can quickly understand business health.

Decision signals

Ranks actionable signals and explains why each one deserves attention.

Customer insight

Uses segmentation and risk indicators to connect aggregate performance to customer behavior.

Scenario model

Lets users test assumptions and estimate profit, ROI, and confidence before committing to an action.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

The interface is a responsive web application built around synthetic business data. The project combines KPI calculations, variance analysis, ranked recommendations, customer segmentation, and an interactive scenario model. Browser-local persistence keeps scenario decisions available during the session.

WHY IT MATTERS

The goal is not more data. It is better action.

Northstar demonstrates how analytics can be designed as a decision product rather than a collection of charts: concise executive storytelling, transparent reasoning, and a direct path from signal to action.
